Highlights

- We formalize the contaminated semi-supervised MTSAD setting with sparse labels.
- Three label paths: anomaly-priority masking, loss bifurcation, gradient reversal.
- CSMAD's asymmetric Teacher-Student masked autoencoder amplifies anomaly discrepancy.
- A contaminated benchmark adds test prefixes to training, exposing labeled anomalies.
- Competitive on [N] datasets under five metrics; graceful decay with label sparsity.
